# Original reef artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for Bubble Grove. The original is `reef-source.png`; the build optimizes it into `reef.webp`.

Prompt: An enchanting luminous underwater garden, portrait 2:3, without text, logos, interface, or fish. Deep blue-teal water with a turquoise shaft of sunlight at the top. Painterly coral, flowing aquatic plants, rounded sea stones, anemones, sea grass, and warm peach coral tips frame the bottom quarter and edges. Keep the upper two thirds and central area quiet open water with atmospheric depth, gentle light rays, and sparse glowing specks. Polished storybook gouache; subdued vegetation lets cream game bubbles stand out.
